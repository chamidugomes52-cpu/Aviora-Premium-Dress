Help & Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

How does Aviora Custom Sizing work? ▼

When ordering any dress, you can select "Custom Fit" or write your bust, waist, hips, and preferred length in the checkout notes or during WhatsApp conversation. Our master atelier will construct the silhouette tailored specifically for you with no alteration extra charges!

How long does Islandwide delivery take? ▼

Standard in-stock dress orders within Colombo arrive in 1-2 business days. Outstation islandwide delivery takes 2-3 business days via secure insured courier. Orders over Rs. 25,000 receive complimentary express shipping.

What payment methods do you accept? ▼

We accept Direct Bank Transfer, Visa / MasterCard credit/debit payments, and Cash On Delivery (COD) for selected islandwide locations. Payment instructions are provided directly during WhatsApp order confirmation.

Can I add my own dress designs to the website? ▼

Yes! The website includes a built-in Boutique Manager Portal (`admin.html`) where the store owner can upload new dress designs, photos directly from device, adjust live prices, and organize categories instantly.
